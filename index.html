01 A little context

Understand the physics.
Test the model.

Over more than ten years, I have developed and validated models for energy storage, erosion and coupled physical systems. My research has brought together experimentalists, industrial partners and space research teams.

I work across mathematical methods, implementation and physical evidence. I check conservation, limiting behaviour and mesh convergence. I make the assumptions clear, so others can understand and use the results.

01Particle impact & blade erosionUniversity of Edinburgh · 2021–2023C++ damage model · Results supplied to MTC+
My roleMSCA Individual Fellow
& sole solver developer
C++PeridynamicsDEM

I developed a hybrid peridynamics-DEM erosion solver within LAMMPS and LIGGGHTS. My work covered formulation, implementation, damage modelling and validation.

I supplied validated damage and erosion results to the Manufacturing Technology Centre. The research supported the study of wind-turbine leading-edge protection.

Read the erosion paper ↗
02Thermochemical energy storageUniversity of Twente · 2019–2021CFD-DEM · Compared with TNO measurements+
My rolePostdoctoral researcher
in thermal & fluid engineering
CFD-DEMOpenFOAMXDEM

I developed coupled CFD-DEM models from particles to packed-bed reactors. The work examined reactions and heat transport in potassium carbonate storage materials.

I investigated contact conduction, radiation and low-pressure convection. Numerical results were compared with TNO measurements. Reactor models supported industrial scale-up decisions.

Read the thermal-conductivity paper ↗
03Coupled physics in microgravityUniversity of Warwick · 2023–2025Coupled field and flow model · Supported experiment design+
My roleResearch Fellow
on the SolarMag project
Elmer FEMOpenFOAMPython

I built a two-way Maxwell-Navier-Stokes model of magnetically induced phase separation in electrochemical water splitting.

The simulation informed magnet configuration, cell geometry and operating requirements for the SolarMag experiment. I developed Python post-processing for planned comparison with flight data. The work brought together chemistry, aerospace and modelling teams.
